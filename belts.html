FROM ZERO TO BLACK BELT MASTERY

WHITE BELT - BEGINNER (10TH GUP)
Beginning of the Taekwondo journey. Learning basic stances, blocks, and front kick (ap chagi). Introduction to dojang etiquette and the five tenets.

YELLOW BELT - BEGINNER (9TH GUP)
Foundation building with Taegeuk Il Jang poomsae, front and roundhouse kicks (dollyo chagi), and one-step sparring fundamentals.

HIGH YELLOW BELT - INTERMEDIATE (8TH GUP)
Advancing basics with Taegeuk Ee Jang poomsae, combination kicks, improved balance, coordination, and spatial awareness.

BLUE BELT - INTERMEDIATE (7TH GUP)
Intermediate development with Taegeuk Sam Jang, side kicks (yeop chagi), and introduction to Kyorugi competitive sparring.

HIGH BLUE BELT - INTERMEDIATE (6TH GUP)
Advanced intermediate with Taegeuk Oh Jang, spinning kicks (dwi dollyo chagi), and competitive sparring techniques.

RED BELT - ADVANCED (5TH GUP)
Advanced training with Taegeuk Yuk Jang and Chil Jang, advanced kicking combinations, and breaking techniques (kyukpa).

HIGH RED BELT - ADVANCED (4TH GUP)
Pre-brown level with Taegeuk Pal Jang, complex poomsae sequences, and advanced self-defense applications.

BROWN BELT - EXPERT (3RD-2ND GUP)
Expert level mastering all Taegeuk forms, tactical sparring strategy, and leadership fundamentals. Teaching assistant responsibilities begin.

HIGH BROWN BELT - EXPERT (1ST GUP)
Expert (1st GUP)

BLACK BELT - MASTER (1ST DAN+)
Peak proficiency and true command of all Taekwondo disciplines. Commitment to lifelong learning, teaching, and embodying the Taekwondo way.
